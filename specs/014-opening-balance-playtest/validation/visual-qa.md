# Visual QA

## Environment

- Godot 4.6.2
- Metal 4.0, Forward+
- Apple M2 Max
- Viewport: 1280×720
- Fixed camera position: `(-0.5, 0.0, -0.5)`
- Zoom: `25.0`

## Result

Pass. The normal-renderer capture visibly contains the rooted road grid, central
Town Hall, nature patches, early home, workplace, shop, and post-war terrace.
The buildable-land boundary is visible, models do not overlap, the camera frames
the full opening layout, and the captured HUD reports Beauty 242. Compact
tutorial guidance was presentation-suppressed for the evidence frame so an
unrelated workstream's temporary copy did not obscure the town.
