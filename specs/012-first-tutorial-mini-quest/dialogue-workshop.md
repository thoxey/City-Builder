**Status**: Placeholder authoring convention approved; final prose intentionally deferred

placeholder copy so implementation can proceed without inventing final prose. Runtime

## Deferred voice anchor

Ambrose's final voice remains a later collaborative writing decision. No voice direction
is selected or inferred in this workstream implementation.

The proposed presentation rhythm is **compact → compact → short full exchange → compact
sequence → short full exchange → compact → short terminal full exchange**. This keeps
basic actions unobtrusive and reserves the dialogue frame for causal lessons.

## Later full-exchange shapes (no final prose yet)

### B05 — Beauty creates Homes demand

1. Ambrose notices the town now looks like somewhere a person might choose to live.
2. The player/guide connects that appeal to the Homes demand meter.
3. Ambrose gives the next actionable instruction or a truthful wait state.

### B09 — Adjacent-home consequence

1. Ambrose reacts to the visibly changed canonical score (never assumes a penalty).
2. The player/guide asks whether closeness is always bad.
3. Ambrose explains the trade-off: compactness has uses, but homes also need relief.
4. The exchange hands off to one measurable decoration/nature repair.

### B15 — Residents at work

1. Ambrose observes an actual resident participating at the workplace.
2. The exchange explains work and earnings in human terms, not as a ledger tutorial.
3. It connects residents with the need/opportunity for a first shop.

### B18 — First-shop handoff

1. Ambrose celebrates a functioning small town without overstating completion.
2. The player/guide notices land will become the next constraint.
3. Ambrose names Sir William as the likely route to more land.
4. The scene ends with the stable handoff; Sir William's actual quest begins only in
   Workstream 2.
